Uses nearby place names from OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) only — nothing is invented — plus the stage length, how much of the route is roads, tracks or off-piste, and the land cover along the line (ESA WorldCover 2021, CC BY 4.0).
Defaults: FIM World Rally-Raid Championship 2026 (Art. 11.2 and 16). Change them to match another organiser's regulations; the stage file carries them, so everyone racing it uses the same rules.

Placement rules of thumb

  • One GPS waypoint every 8–20 km: more where the route offers choices, fewer on obvious tracks.
  • Keep points at least ~300 m apart so their circles don't overlap (the builder warns you).
  • On long featureless legs, give the navigator something to check the trip meter against (a Route point or a WPV) between hidden waypoints.
  • Every village gets a speed zone: DZ ~1–1.5 km before it, FZ ~1–1.5 km after. Close every zone before the next DZ and before the ASS.
  • Off-piste legs: end the roadbook note with the heading to hold, e.g. “Hold CAP 247” (the list shows the CAP leaving each point).
