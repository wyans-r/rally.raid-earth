rally.raid Earth for BeamNG.drive

Dakar-style rally raid for BeamNG.Earth: a GPS with CAP and odometer that follows the FIM reveal rules, a scrolling roadbook, timed stages with penalties, and a recce mode for refining notes as you drive.

Download rallyraid_earth.zip
Build of 17 Nov 2026 · includes 15 example stages. The examples and their roadbooks are auto-generated by the stage builder; hand-made (curated) examples are still to come.
This is an early build. Requires BeamNG.drive with the BeamNG.Earth mod installed and working. The GPS and Roadbook apps have been driven on a full stage in game; the features marked EXPERIMENTAL / UNTESTED below have not been tested in game yet.

Experimental / untested

These are in this download but have not been tested in game yet. They may not work, or may behave oddly. Please report what you find (see Something broken?).

  • EXPERIMENTAL / UNTESTED Repair: the rally.raid Earth Repair app and the repair menu (repair times, bivouac repairs, reset penalties).
  • EXPERIMENTAL / UNTESTED Recover car: put the car back on the route for a time penalty (two steps: RECOVER, then CONFIRM).
  • EXPERIMENTAL / UNTESTED Crash resume: carry on a stage after the game crashed (RESUME, then CONFIRM RESUME).
  • EXPERIMENTAL / UNTESTED Planner: the rally.raid Earth Planner app (waypoints, roadbook boxes and recording your driven line in game).
  • EXPERIMENTAL / UNTESTED GPS MENU: the MENU button panel on the GPS app that opens Repair, Recover, Resume and the Planner.
  • EXPERIMENTAL / UNTESTED Recce mode: the RECCE panel on the Roadbook app (drop, confirm, move, retag and delete boxes, undo).
  • EXPERIMENTAL / UNTESTED Your own keys in the apps: the apps showing the key you bound in Options > Controls instead of the default.
  • EXPERIMENTAL / UNTESTED Ctrl+Shift keys: not yet checked whether a Ctrl+Shift key (for example Ctrl+Shift+Up or Ctrl+Shift+Home) also fires BeamNG’s own action for the plain key. If it does, rebind it.

Install

  1. Close BeamNG.drive.
  2. Paste %LOCALAPPDATA%\BeamNG\BeamNG.drive\current\mods\ into the File Explorer address bar and press Enter.
  3. Put rallyraid_earth.zip in that folder without unzipping it. When updating, replace the old zip (keep the same filename).
  4. Start the game, check the mod is on in the Mod Manager, load BeamNG.Earth and spawn a car.
  5. Press Esc, open UI Apps, choose Add app and add rally.raid Earth GPS and rally.raid Earth Roadbook (category "rally.raid Earth"). Also add rally.raid Earth Repair and rally.raid Earth Planner EXPERIMENTAL / UNTESTED if you want those menus. Drag and resize them to taste.

Race a stage

  1. On the GPS app press STAGES (or BOOK on the roadbook app), click a stage and press LOAD, or double-click it.
  2. Press TP TO DSS to teleport to the start, or drive there; the GPS points to the start until the stage begins.
  3. Drive out of the start circle to start the clock, then navigate by roadbook. The GPS shows waypoint distance and heading only when you get close, as in a real rally.

Keys

Only the keys you need while driving have defaults (rebind them in Options > Controls > Bindings, search "rally.raid"; the apps show the key you set EXPERIMENTAL / UNTESTED):

Ctrl+Shift+Uptrip + (hold to repeat)
Ctrl+Shift+Downtrip − (hold to repeat)
Ctrl+Shift+LeftW− (back to a missed waypoint)
Ctrl+Shift+RightW+ (skip waypoint)
Ctrl+Shift+Preset the partial odometer
Ctrl+Shift+Homestart the 5 s countdown (stand in the start circle)
Ctrl+Shift+PageUproadbook box up (the trip is not changed)
Ctrl+Shift+PageDownroadbook box down (the trip is not changed)
Ctrl+Shift+Endsync the trip to the box on screen (“I am at this box”)

Trip step size (10 / 100 / 1000 m) is the 10m button on the GPS app, not a key. Trip reset and start a new rally have no default keys; you can bind them in Options > Controls.

Buttons (no default key)

Everything else is a button in the apps (the GPS app’s MENU is EXPERIMENTAL / UNTESTED), so it cannot clash with BeamNG’s own keys. You can still give any of these a key in Options > Controls; the apps then show it.

Make your own stages

Plot a route in the stage builder (the Examples button loads the 15 auto-generated samples), export the stage .json, then make its roadbook in the roadbook editor. Put stage files in %LOCALAPPDATA%\BeamNG\BeamNG.drive\current\rallyraid\stages\ and roadbooks in ...\current\rallyraid\roadbooks\ (create the folders).

Something broken?

Press ~ in game to open the console and screenshot any red errors, then send the screenshot and what you were doing to wyans (reply where you found this download).
