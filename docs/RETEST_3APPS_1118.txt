rally.raid Earth - THREE HUD APPS retest (18 Nov 2026)
Zip: rallyraid_earth_3apps1118_test.zip (TEST build, not a release; built with --include-events --include-rally --include-rally-events)
Branch three-apps-1118 (local only, not pushed).

What changed:
- GPS app = the rally computer, with the REPAIR panel built in (MENU > REPAIR: everything the Repair app had) and the
  RALLY panel (MENU > RALLY: everything the Rally app had, EXPERIMENTAL).
- Planner app = the planner, plus a RECCE button in its bar: the recce controls that were in the Roadbook app.
- Roadbook app = the reader, as before, minus its RECCE button and panel (a small RECCE tag shows in its head while recce is on).
- The Repair, Rally and Event apps are gone from the zip. No new keys: still the same 9 default keys.

Before you start (2 min)
1. Close the game. Remove any older rally.raid Earth zip from the mods folder and put rallyraid_earth_3apps1118_test.zip in.
   Expected: only one rally.raid Earth zip in mods.
2. Start the game, load BeamNG.Earth, spawn a car. Open the console (~) and keep it handy.
   Expected: no red rally.raid Earth errors in the console.

A. The app list and the layouts (4 min)
3. Esc > UI Apps > Add app, search "rally.raid".
   Expected: exactly three: rally.raid Earth GPS, rally.raid Earth Roadbook, rally.raid Earth Planner. No Repair, Rally or Event app.
   (If you still see the old Repair / Rally apps on screen from an old layout, remove them: they are not in this zip.)
4. Add the GPS app. In it press MENU > RALLY LAYOUT.
   Expected: GPS + Roadbook on screen (nothing else from the mod). Console: "app layout -> rallyRaidEarth".
5. MENU > PLANNER LAYOUT.
   Expected: GPS + Roadbook + Planner. Console: "app layout -> rallyRaidEarthPlanner".
6. MENU > MY LAYOUT.
   Expected: back to the layout you had before. Then go back to PLANNER LAYOUT for the rest of the test.

B. REPAIR in the GPS app (6 min)
7. With no stage running: GPS MENU. Look at the row "Repairs, spares, bivouac time (...)".
   Expected: the row is there with a REPAIR button; the brackets show resets (and spares mid-stage).
8. Press REPAIR.
   Expected: a REPAIR panel covers the GPS: a header "REPAIR" with an X, the repair status bar (CLOSE, RESETS n/3) and
   "No repairs here ..." text. Console: "cmd repairOpen (gps)" and "repair menu opened (none, GPS app on screen)".
9. Press CLOSE in the panel.
   Expected: the panel goes, the GPS computer shows again. Console: "cmd repairClose".
10. Load a stage (STAGES > pick > LOAD), TP TO DSS, START, drive 1-2 km, stop the car. Hit something first if you can
    (a flat tyre or a bent radiator is ideal). GPS MENU > REPAIR.
    Expected: the mid-stage list: change wheel FL/FR/RL/RR (with spare count), suspension, radiator, with minutes; the
    REPAIR button is greyed until you tick something.
11. Tick one item, press REPAIR in the panel.
    Expected: the repair runs, the time is added as a penalty (shown in the GPS penalties), spares go down by one for a wheel,
    the panel closes after.
12. Panel X (top right) instead of CLOSE, on a second open.
    Expected: closes the same way.
13. Finish the stage (or abort it) so the bivouac opens. GPS MENU > REPAIR.
    Expected: the bivouac list (whole-car repair, engine change with its penalty), the bivouac time used / budget / left.
14. Only if you have bound a key to "rally.raid Earth: repair menu": press it.
    Expected: the REPAIR panel opens on the GPS by itself. Skip if no key is bound.

C. RECOVER and crash RESUME still in the GPS (3 min)
15. During a stage, GPS MENU > RECOVER, then CONFIRM.
    Expected: the same as before (penalty shown, car put back on the route). The REPAIR panel's RECOVER CAR button does the same.
16. (Optional, 5 min more) Kill the game mid-stage, relaunch, load the same stage.
    Expected: the GPS MENU opens with the crash save, RESUME then CONFIRM RESUME works.

D. RALLY in the GPS (EXPERIMENTAL, 4 min)
17. GPS MENU.
    Expected: a row "Rally mode EXPERIMENTAL" with a RALLY button.
18. Press RALLY.
    Expected: a RALLY panel over the GPS with the list of rallies (Atlantic to Atlas 2027). Console: "cmd rallyList (gps)".
19. Pick the rally (LOAD), then DRIVE or TELEPORT for the first liaison. Close the panel with X.
    Expected: while a liaison runs, a yellow "LIAISON ... km, CAP ..., LEFT ..." line shows on the GPS with a RALLY button
    that opens the panel again.

E. RECCE in the Planner (8 min)
20. Abort any stage and make sure no stage is loaded (restart the map if needed). In the Planner bar press RECCE.
    Expected: a RECCE panel under the bar: "No stage loaded in the game ... Load one with STAGES in the GPS app, then
    RECCE ON." The RECCE ON button is greyed.
21. Load a stage in the GPS (STAGES > LOAD), e.g. SS1 Moab Sand Flats.
    Expected: the RECCE panel now names the stage and its number of boxes; RECCE ON is active.
22. Press RECCE ON.
    Expected: the full recce controls (the same as the old Roadbook RECCE panel): NEAR n, the arrows, AT TRIP, KM = TRIP,
    -10 / +10, CAP HERE, + BOX HERE, CHANGES n, REPORT, MERGE, OFF. The Planner bar button reads "● RECCE". The Roadbook
    head shows "· RECCE". Console: "cmd recce (planner)".
23. Drive next to a box. Press the right arrow twice, then AT TRIP.
    Expected: "SEL n" follows the arrows; AT TRIP picks the box at the trip meter.
24. Press KM = TRIP, then +10.
    Expected: the selected box's km changes to the trip, then 10 m more; the Roadbook app shows the new km at once.
25. Press CAP HERE.
    Expected: the box CAP becomes the car heading; the Roadbook shows it at once.
26. Press + BOX HERE, pick a symbol, tap two note presets (e.g. ROUGH, JUNCTION NOT VISIBLE), SAVE.
    Expected: the presets fill the notes; after SAVE the new box shows in the Roadbook app at once with those notes.
27. Open CHANGES n. Press UNDO on one row.
    Expected: only that change is taken back (the Roadbook shows the old value again).
28. Press MERGE.
    Expected: a MERGE PREVIEW line (how many changes, warnings). Press CANCEL: it closes, nothing written.
29. Press REPORT.
    Expected: "REPORT: /rallyraid/recce/<stage>.report.txt" shows; the .txt and .json are in your BeamNG user folder.
30. Press MERGE, then CONFIRM MERGE.
    Expected: the roadbook is written (a new rev), CHANGES 0.
31. Press OFF.
    Expected: recce is off, the panel shows RECCE ON again, the Roadbook head tag goes away.
32. Press RECCE in the Planner bar.
    Expected: the RECCE panel hides. The rest of the Planner (PLANNER button, NEW STAGE HERE / EDIT LOADED STAGE) still works.

F. The Roadbook app is a reader (2 min)
33. Look at the Roadbook app's buttons.
    Expected: ▲ ▼ SYNC ↻ BOOK only, no RECCE button, no recce panel. Box up / down / sync keys work as before.

G. Planner basics still fine (3 min)
34. Planner bar > PLANNER > EDIT LOADED STAGE (with a stage loaded).
    Expected: the loaded stage opens in the planner editor, as on 17 Nov.

H. Report back (2 min)
35. Copy the console lines that start with "[rally.raid Earth]" (or the whole log) and screenshots of: the GPS REPAIR panel,
    the GPS RALLY panel, the Planner RECCE panel with recce on, and the Roadbook while recce is on.

Rough total: about 35-40 min (about 45 min with the optional crash test).
