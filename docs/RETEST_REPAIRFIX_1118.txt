rally.raid Earth - REPAIR + RECOVER FIX retest (18 Nov 2026)
Zip: rallyraid_earth_repairfix1118_test.zip (TEST build, not a release; built with --include-events --include-rally --include-rally-events)
Branch three-apps-1118 (local only, not pushed). Same three apps as the last zip; only repairs and RECOVER changed.

What changed (what you reported -> what was done):
- "replacing wheels didn't work" / "the tires immediately punctured again": after the reset the old code put EVERY old
  node position and beam length back, including the hub and the new tyre's own beams, so the new tyre was pulled onto
  the old bent spot and torn flat. Now nothing of a changed wheel (tyre, rim, its hub nodes) is put back.
- "fixing the suspension broke the steering": the old code also put back the steering rack's old length (a "hydro"
  beam: the part that moves when you steer), freezing it. Now hydros and tyre-pressure beams are never touched, and
  no old dent that pulls on a newly repaired part is put back.
- "recovery also completely fixed my vehicle": RECOVER no longer resets the car. It lifts it, sets it upright on the
  route and stops it (the game's own "recover upright" call). All damage stays. Penalty and limits are unchanged:
  +15 min first, +30 min after; 3 per rally, 1 per stage, none within 1 km of the ASS.
- New console lines around every repair and recovery (listed below), all starting with [rally.raid Earth].

Where the log lines are:
- In game: the console, key ~ (tilde). Type "rally.raid" in its filter box to see only ours.
- On disk: <BeamNG user folder>/current/beamng.log (for example Documents\BeamNG.drive\current\beamng.log, or
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\BeamNG.drive\<version>\beamng.log on newer versions). If anything fails, send this file.

Total: about 20 min (15-30).

Before you start (2 min)
1. Close the game. Remove the older rally.raid Earth zip from mods, put rallyraid_earth_repairfix1118_test.zip in.
   Start the game, BeamNG.Earth, spawn a car (a pickup or any car with a normal steering rack), add the GPS app.
   Expected: no red rally.raid Earth errors in the console.
2. Load a stage, TP TO DSS, START, drive about 1 km.

A. Change a wheel (4 min)
3. Puncture a tyre (hit a rock or kerb hard) and bend that corner a bit if you can. Stop. GPS MENU > REPAIR, tick
   "Change wheel" for that corner, REPAIR.
   Expected: the wheel is new and round, the tyre is inflated and STAYS inflated when you drive off (drive 500 m,
   steer both ways). Other damage (body, other flat tyres) stays. +2 min, one spare used.
   Console, in order:
     repair N requested (stage mode): Change wheel FL = 2 min ...
     car report (before repair N): broken beams .., deformed beams .., flat tyres FL ...
     repair N ready: wheels changed FL, flat tyres before FL; kept for after the reset: ... skippedTouchesRepair=..
     repair N: reset in place with ...            (no FAILED)
     repair N done (...): put back ...; flat tyres now none (or only the corners you did not change)
     car report (after repair N): ...
     repair N, 1 s later: flat tyres ..., a changed tyre flat again: none, broken beams in the repaired parts: 0
   If a changed tyre does go flat again you get a note on screen and a W line "... flat again: FL". Send the log.
4. Repeat once on another corner (or the same one with the second spare).
   Expected: same as 3.

B. Suspension repair keeps the steering (4 min)
5. Damage one front corner's suspension (a hard hit on the front wheel). Stop. REPAIR > Suspension for that corner.
   Expected: the corner is straight again and the STEERING WORKS normally both ways (wheels turn with the steering
   wheel, the car drives straight with the wheel centred). +18 min.
   Console: the same lines as in 3 with "Suspension FL" (or the corner); "1 s later ... broken beams in the repaired
   parts: 0".

C. Radiator, if you have one bent (2 min, optional)
6. REPAIR > Radiator.
   Expected: radiator fixed, the rest of the damage kept, steering fine. +12 min. Same console lines.

D. RECOVER keeps the damage (5 min)
7. Damage the car clearly (bent body, a flat tyre) and roll it onto its side or roof, or drive 40+ m off the route.
   Note what is broken. Stop. GPS MENU > RECOVER, then CONFIRM.
   Expected: the car is lifted, set upright on the route facing the right way and stops. ALL the damage is still
   there: same dents, same flat tyre, same fuel. +15 min (first of the rally), recoveries 1/3.
   Console:
     car recovery requested: to km ... ; penalty +15 min
     car report (before recovery N): broken beams A, deformed beams B, flat tyres ...
     car recovery: spawn.safeTeleport(pos ..., resetVehicle = false) ok
     car recovery N done: ...; landed .. m from the target, .. deg off the route heading
     car report (after recovery N): broken beams about A, deformed beams about B, same flat tyres
   (Compare the two "car report" lines: the counts should be about the same. If "after" shows 0 broken beams, it
   repaired: send the log.)
8. Limits unchanged (1 min): on the same stage press RECOVER again.
   Expected: refused (second recovery on this stage), RETIRE STAGE offered. Cancel.

E. Bivouac whole-car repair (3 min, optional)
9. Finish or retire the stage, then GPS MENU > REPAIR in the bivouac: pick the whole-car repair.
   Expected: the car is fully fixed (that one is meant to), steering fine, tyres round. Console: "repair N requested
   (bivouac mode)", "ready", "reset in place with", "done", "1 s later ... flat again: none".

Known limits (not bugs in this build):
- RECOVER far from the route (more than 1.5 km, or where BeamNG.Earth has not loaded the world) still goes through
  BeamNG.Earth's teleport, which resets the car. The damage is captured when you press CONFIRM and put back after,
  so it is close but not exact.
- Game-crash resume must reset the car and puts the saved damage back the same approximate way. Unchanged.
