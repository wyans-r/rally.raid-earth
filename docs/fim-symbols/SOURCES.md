# Roadbook lexicon sources

We read these public documents (downloaded 16 Nov 2026) to learn how real
rally-raid roadbooks are drawn. Their wording and artwork belong to their
publishers. **This repository does not copy them**: no PDFs, page scans or text
extracts are stored here. Our symbols in `stage-builder/roadbook-render.js` are
our own SVG drawings that follow the general meaning; our summary is in
[`NOTES.md`](NOTES.md).
